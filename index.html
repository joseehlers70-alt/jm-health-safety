Ask us a question
Do you assist businesses outside Gauteng?

Yes. JM Health and Safety Consultants is based in Moreletapark and advertises nationwide services. Contact us with your location and requirements so we can discuss the arrangements.

Can you prepare files to a client's requirements?

Yes. Site-specific health and safety files can be prepared according to the specifications supplied by your client or project team.

Can you help with PPE, medicals and training?

Yes. We offer PPE supply, arrange occupational health medical examinations and arrange accredited health and safety training. Ask us about the options relevant to your team.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the enquiry form below or contact Johannes or Prince directly. Share the service you need, your location and relevant project details, and our team can discuss the scope and quotation.

Does a safety file guarantee legal compliance?

Safety documentation is one part of health and safety management. Compliance depends on applicable legal duties, the work performed, and how measures are implemented and maintained. Speak to a qualified consultant about your specific needs.
